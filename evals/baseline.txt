                According to the study, protein intakes at amounts greater than ~1.6 g/kg/day do not further contribute to RET-induced gains in fat-free mass (FFM). However, with protein supplementation, protein intakes of 1.62 g/kg/day resulted in no further gains in FFM.

                * Fat-free mass (FFM) by 0.30 kg
                * Muscle size, as measured by muscle fibre cross-sectional area (CSA), by 310 µm² and mid-femur CSA by 7.2 mm²
                
                The impact of protein supplementation on gains in FFM was reduced with increasing age (-0.01 kg) and was more effective in resistance-trained individuals (0.75 kg).
                
                [morton_2017]
